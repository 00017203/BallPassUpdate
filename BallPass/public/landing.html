How BallPass Works

Simple, secure, and transparent in just 4 steps.

📝
STEP 01

Create Your Account

Sign up as a Buyer or Seller. Your account is verified and secured with encrypted credentials.

🔍
STEP 02

Browse or List

Buyers browse verified listings by match, city, or price. Sellers list their ticket with seat details in seconds.

🏟
STEP 03

Select Your Seat

Choose your exact seat on the interactive stadium map, preview your view in 3D, then confirm your purchase.

✅
STEP 04

Enjoy the Match

Your purchase is recorded and confirmed. Print your ticket and show it at the gate.

Built for Trust

Every feature is designed to protect buyers and sellers.

🔐

Encrypted Passwords

All passwords are hashed with bcrypt — never stored as plain text.

✔

Verified Accounts

Users must verify via a 6-digit code before buying or selling. No anonymous transactions.

💺

Seat-Level Transparency

Every listing shows the exact section and seat number. No vague descriptions.

🧾

Transaction Records

Every purchase is permanently recorded in the database with a unique booking reference.

🖨

Printable Tickets

Buyers receive a printable ticket with booking reference immediately after purchase.

📊

Price Transparency

Market analytics show real average prices so buyers can spot unfair listings.
